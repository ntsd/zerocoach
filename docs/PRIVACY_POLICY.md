# ZeroCoach — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 19, 2026*

ZeroCoach ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you use our mobile application (**ZeroCoach**, `tech.zerocoach.app`) and related services.

---

### 1. Privacy-First & On-Device Processing
ZeroCoach is architected as an offline-first, privacy-focused intelligent athletic coach. Wherever supported by your hardware, AI analysis and coaching logic run **locally on-device** using embedded models. Your conversations and workout recommendations remain on your phone.

---

### 2. Information We Access and Process

#### A. Health and Fitness Data (Apple Health & Android Health Connect)
With your explicit consent, ZeroCoach requests read and write access to health platforms:
- **Metrics accessed**: Daily step counts, active & total energy burned (calories), resting heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), sleep duration and stages, weight, and height.
- **Why we need this data**: Health metrics are utilized strictly to calculate your recovery readiness score, estimate muscle strain/fatigue, and tailor daily adaptive workouts.
- **Data Sharing & Selling**: **We do not sell, rent, or trade your health data.** Health data is never used for advertising, marketing, or data broker purposes.

#### B. Camera Access
ZeroCoach may request permission to use your device's camera:
- **Purpose**: To allow you to take photos of your meals or nutritional labels for automated macronutrient tracking, or scanning recovery-related media.
- **Processing**: Camera imagery is used solely for the feature you initiate and is not stored or shared for external advertising.

#### C. Audio & Voice (Optional)
If you interact with the voice coach feature powered by text-to-speech or voice recognition:
- Audio clips are processed strictly to fulfill the immediate spoken coaching interaction.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services
ZeroCoach may interact with third-party service providers solely to provide core application features:
- **AI / Voice Synthesis** (e.g. ElevenLabs API, cloud LLM fallback if configured by the user): When cloud processing is enabled, only prompt context necessary to generate the coaching response or audio is transmitted securely over HTTPS.
- We do not integrate third-party ad trackers or data brokers.

---

### 4. Data Retention & User Control
- **Local Storage**: All your workout plans, logs, and telemetry reside locally on your device in secure sandbox storage.
- **Revoking Permissions**: You can revoke access to Apple Health or Android Health Connect at any time via your device’s system settings (Apple Health settings or Android Health Connect / Settings > Privacy).
- **Data Deletion**: Uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored data. If cloud accounts or remote sync features are ever activated in future updates, you will have a direct option in settings to delete your account and associated data.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy
ZeroCoach does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.
