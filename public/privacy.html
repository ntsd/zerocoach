Privacy Policy

ZeroCoach

Last updated: October 19, 2026

ZeroCoach ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines how information is handled when using our mobile application (ZeroCoach, tech.zerocoach.app).

1. Privacy-First & On-Device Processing

ZeroCoach is engineered with an on-device first philosophy. Athletic intelligence, training volume analysis, and conversational coaching models execute directly on your mobile device whenever supported by hardware. Your conversations and workout telemetry stay on your device.

2. Health & Fitness Data (Apple Health & Android Health Connect)

With your explicit permission, ZeroCoach accesses health platform metrics including:

  • Steps, Distance & Calories Burned (Active & Total)
  • Heart Rate, Resting Heart Rate & Heart Rate Variability (HRV)
  • Sleep analysis (duration and sleep stages)
  • Weight & Height

Strict Use: This data is used solely to compute your athletic recovery readiness, prevent overtraining, and adapt workout intensity. We do NOT sell, lease, or monetize your health data or share it with third-party advertisers.

3. Camera Permission

Camera access is used optionally and exclusively when you choose to snap pictures of meals for nutritional macronutrient breakdown or scanning fitness data.

4. Third-Party Integrations

When external services are enabled (such as high-fidelity voice coaching through ElevenLabs API or cloud fallback LLMs), only the text prompts required to produce the response are transmitted over secure TLS encryption. No health identifiers are linked or transferred for ad targeting.

5. Data Deletion & Retention

ZeroCoach stores your workout history in the local device sandbox. You can revoke permissions at any time via your device's system settings (Apple Health or Health Connect) or delete your data simply by uninstalling the application.
